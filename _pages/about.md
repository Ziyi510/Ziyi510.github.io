I received my bachelor's degree from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) with Highest Distinction. During my undergraduate studies, I worked with [Prof. Lihui Chen](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/entities/person/Chen-Lihui). I am currently a master's student in Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore (NUS), where I am a member of the [SHOW Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/showlab) working with [Anno Chen](https://chenanno.github.io/).

My research interests lie in large language models, AI agents, and embodied agents, with an emphasis on bridging language models and interactive embodied systems.

In Spring 2025, I spent a semester on exchange at Virginia Tech.

In Summer 2025, I was a research intern at Rice University.
