I am currently a master's student in Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore (NUS), where I am a member of the [SHOW Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/showlab) working with [Anno Yanzhe Chen](https://chenanno.github.io/).
{: .intro__masters}

Prior to this, I obtained my bachelor's degree with Highest Distinction in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), where I worked with [Prof. Lihui Chen](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/entities/person/Chen-Lihui).

My research interests lie in large language models, AI agents, and embodied agents.

In Spring 2025, I spent a semester on exchange at Virginia Tech.<br>
In Summer 2025, I was a research intern at Rice University.

You may refer to my [CV](/files/CV.pdf) for more details.
